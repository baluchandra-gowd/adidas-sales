Adidas Sales Dashboard
📊 Project Overview
The Adidas Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard
created to analyze Adidas sales performance across different states,
cities, regions, product categories, sales methods, and gender types.
The dashboard provides a clear view of overall sales and units sold
while allowing users to filter the analysis by Gender Type and
State.
🎯 Objectives
•	Analyze total Adidas sales performance.
•	Track total units sold.
•	Compare sales across different states and cities.
•	Analyze sales by region.
•	Compare product categories across different sales methods.
•	Provide interactive filtering for better business analysis.
•	Identify high-performing locations and sales channels.

📈 Dashboard Visuals
1. Count of Product Category by Sales Method and Product Category
A stacked bar chart compares the number of products across different
sales methods such as:
•	Online
•	Outlet
•	In-store
The visualization also breaks the results down by product category.
2. Total Sales by State
A line chart shows the total sales generated by each state and helps
identify states with higher and lower sales performance.
3. Total Sales by City
A column chart compares sales performance across cities and highlights
the cities contributing the most to total sales.
4. Total Sales by Region
A pie chart displays the percentage contribution of each region to
overall sales.
5. Gender Type Filter
Users can filter the dashboard based on:
•	Men
•	Women
6. State Filter
Users can select individual states to perform more detailed sales
analysis.
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
•	Power BI -- Dashboard development and visualization
•	Power Query -- Data cleaning and transformation
•	DAX -- KPI and analytical calculations
•	Excel / CSV -- Data source and preparation
🔍 Key Insights
•	Total sales shown in the dashboard are 120M.
•	Total units sold are approximately 2M.
•	Online sales have the highest product-category count among the
displayed sales methods.
•	Sales performance varies significantly across states and cities.
•	The regional sales distribution shows different contributions from
each region.
•	Interactive filters make it possible to analyze performance by
gender and state.
